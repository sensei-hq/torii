---
name: research-method
description: Compare options on stated criteria and keep evidence apart from inference
activate_on: [research, investigate, compare, comparison, evaluate, trade-off, tradeoff, pros and cons, alternatives, versus]
---
When researching or comparing:
- State the question and the criteria that decide it before gathering anything.
- For each option, record the evidence for and against it on every criterion, and where that evidence comes from.
- Mark each claim as established, likely or speculative.
- Close with a recommendation and the condition that would change it.
