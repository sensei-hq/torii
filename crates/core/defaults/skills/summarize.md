---
name: summarize
description: Condense material to what the reader needs, without losing what matters
activate_on: [summarize, summarise, summary, tl;dr, tldr, recap, condense, key points, executive brief]
---
When summarizing:
- Lead with the single most important conclusion, in one sentence.
- Keep every figure, date, name and decision that changes what the reader would do; drop the rest.
- Preserve the source's uncertainty: never turn a "may" into a "will".
- Respect any requested length; without one, aim for a tenth of the source or five bullets, whichever is shorter.
